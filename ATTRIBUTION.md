# Attribution

Free Family Display ships photographs that other people made and licensed for
reuse. The software is Apache 2.0 (see `LICENSE`); **these images are not** —
each one stays under its own licence, listed below.

Generated from `apps/web/public/wallpapers/manifest.json` and
`apps/web/public/login-photos/credits.json`, which are also what the app reads
to print the credit on screen. Regenerate with `npm run attribution`.
